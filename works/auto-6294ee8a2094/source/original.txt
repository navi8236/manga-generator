A BETTER WAY TO CARE
A ten-page English healthcare comic for Welbuk (six pages in the original brief; one page added in the 2026-11-11 revision and three in the 2026-11-12 revision). The comic is written for clinics and hospitals, Welbuk's customers: it shows what the clinic saves as well as what the patient gains.

This is a fictional illustrated onboarding journey, not a claim that every depicted feature is presently available. Mark future referral-network experiences as vision.

CAST
Dr. Ananya: Indian female clinic owner in her late 30s, calm, confident, caring; teal kurta beneath white coat.
Meera: Indian female receptionist in her late 20s, friendly and capable, blue kurta.
Ravi: Indian male patient in his mid-30s, warm, approachable, light green shirt.
Nurse: Indian female clinic nurse, friendly and efficient (added in the 2026-11-11 revision).
Two other patients (added in the 2026-11-11 revision): an elderly man who checks in at the front desk, and a young woman who checks in on the Welbuk Health app.
Pharmacist and lab technician (added in the 2026-11-12 revision): they receive the orders after the visit.

ART DIRECTION
Full colour, approachable Indian slice-of-life manga, expressive faces, precise hands, restrained blue and teal accents, warm natural clinic, consistent character appearances. Western left-to-right reading order. Vary panel sizes. Make panel art WITHOUT speech bubbles or text; compose English dialogue separately. Avoid real patient data, unsupported medical claims, and portraying future vision as launched capability.
