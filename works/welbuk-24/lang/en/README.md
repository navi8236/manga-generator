# English voice-over (narrator only, no music)

- `tour-en-voice.wav|mp3`, `reel-en-voice.wav|mp3`: the full English narration, already positioned on the films'
  timelines (start at 0:00 of the video), 48 kHz mono. Use as a timing guide when recording other languages.
- `lines/` (and `english-lines.zip`): one clip per line, named by the IDs in `../TRANSCRIPT-en.md`
  (tour-intro ... tour-close, reel-r01 ... reel-r18).
- The music-only tracks are in `../../video-kit/<film>/audio/<film>-music.m4a`; the full English mixes are
  `../../video-kit/<film>/audio/<film>-en.m4a`.
