tour-intro	Welbuk is one connected platform for clinics and hospitals. Welbuk Practice runs the clinic. Welbuk Care supports every member of the care team. And Welbuk Health keeps patients and families connected.
tour-appointments	Appointments come together in one place: online booking, doctor schedules, slot allocation and walk-ins.
tour-queue	A live queue sorts every visit as prior, walk-in or emergency, so urgent patients are seen first, and every update reaches the whole team.
tour-checkin	Patients register with a QR code and give consent digitally, or check in with help at the front desk, straight into the queue.
tour-careteam	Welbuk Care gives each role its own workflow. Nurses record vitals, doctors see their consultation queue, and notifications keep the team in step.
tour-emr	Every consultation starts with context: history, allergies, vitals and structured SOAP notes.
tour-scribe	With the patient's consent, Care Scribe transcribes the conversation, separates the speakers and drafts a clinical summary. The doctor reviews every word before signing.
tour-spec-intro	Specialty tools are built in.
tour-dental	Dentists chart findings and plan treatment on an odontogram.
tour-eye	Eye clinics record visual acuity and refraction.
tour-derm	Dermatology maps body sites and compares clinical photos over time.
tour-paeds	And paediatrics tracks growth charts, vaccinations and screening.
tour-rx	Digital prescriptions carry medication information, and every issued prescription stays on record.
tour-pharmacy	The pharmacy tracks batches, expiry dates and stock levels, and dispenses first-expiry, first-out.
tour-lab	Lab orders move from sample collection to results and the doctor's review, without lost paperwork.
tour-referral	Referrals to specialists and external partners stay connected, and every one can be tracked.
tour-billing	Billing handles invoices, applicable GST, every payment method, receipts and balances.
tour-dayend	At the end of the day, payments and refunds are reconciled, with GST-aware reports for clear financial oversight.
tour-facilities	Multiple facilities run from one platform. Welbuk Cloud is available today. For on-premises, hybrid or air-gapped AI, let's talk.
tour-hrms	For the people behind care, there are staff records, attendance, leave management and role-based access control.
tour-health	After the visit, Welbuk Health keeps patients connected, with visit history, prescriptions, reports, reminders and follow-ups.
tour-family	Families manage health together, with guardian links, vaccination records and growth tracking.
tour-ahead	Looking ahead, our goal is one trusted network of clinics, specialists, pharmacies and laboratories, sharing records only with the patient's permission.
tour-close	Welbuk. Technology supports the work. People deliver the care. Building a connected healthcare ecosystem.
