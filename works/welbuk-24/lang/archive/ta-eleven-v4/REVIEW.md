# Tamil voice-over: review sheet

For a native Tamil speaker. Please check each line for natural wording, correct terms and pronunciation in the audio
(`video-kit/tour/audio/tour-ta.m4a`, `video-kit/reel/audio/reel-ta.m4a`, or the web player). Mark changes in the
"Tamil" column; lines can be re-voiced individually for well under one credit each.

- Voice: ElevenLabs Eleven v4 (preset female voice "Anika") via Higgsfield. The English narrator could not be
  cloned on this plan, and Seed Audio (used for English) does not speak Tamil.
- Product and technical terms are kept in English as clinic staff say them; captions show them in English letters.
- "Speed" is how much a line was sped up (pitch kept) to fit the picture; above x1.15 may sound brisk.
- Lines marked FLYER are additions from the client flyer that the English voice-over does not have yet.
