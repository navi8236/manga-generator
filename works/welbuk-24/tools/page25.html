Everything Welbuk does

One connected platform for clinic operations, healthcare teams and patients.

Appointments & front desk

  • Online booking with doctor schedules
  • Slot allocation for booked visits and walk-ins
  • Prior, Walk-in and Emergency queue priorities
  • Live queue and token display
  • QR check-in with digital consent
  • Assisted registration at reception

Clinical care

  • Electronic medical records: history, allergies, medications, vitals
  • Vitals recording by nursing staff
  • Doctor consultation queue
  • SOAP-style clinical notes
  • Digital prescriptions and prescription history
  • Specialist referrals with status tracking

SpeakADoc voice documentation

  • Patient consent before recording
  • Voice transcription with speaker separation
  • AI-drafted clinical summary
  • Doctor review before the record is completed

Specialty workflows

  • Dentistry: odontogram, findings, treatment planning
  • Ophthalmology: visual acuity, refraction, eye charts
  • Dermatology: body-site mapping, clinical photos, visit comparison
  • Paediatrics: WHO growth charts, vaccinations, screening

Pharmacy & laboratory

  • Batch-wise inventory with expiry tracking
  • FEFO dispensing and live stock levels
  • Lab orders linked to the patient
  • Sample collection and result review

Billing & finance

  • Invoicing with applicable GST
  • QR and other payment methods, receipts
  • Balance tracking and refunds
  • Day-end reconciliation and financial reports

Staff & administration

  • Role-based workflows and access control
  • Staff records and attendance (HRMS)
  • Leave and department management
  • Notifications across the team
  • Multi-facility operations

Patients & families: Welbuk Health

  • Visit history, prescriptions and reports
  • Follow-up reminders on consented channels
  • Family profiles with guardian links
  • Children's vaccination and growth records
