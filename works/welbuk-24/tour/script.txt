Welbuk is one connected platform for clinics and hospitals. Welbuk Practice runs the clinic. Welbuk Care supports every member of the care team. And Welbuk Health keeps patients and families connected.
Appointments come together in one place: online booking, doctor schedules, slot allocation and walk-ins.
A live queue sorts every visit as prior, walk-in or emergency, so urgent patients are seen first, and every update reaches the whole team.
Patients register with a QR code and give consent digitally, or check in with help at the front desk, straight into the queue.
Welbuk Care gives each role its own workflow. Nurses record vitals, doctors see their consultation queue, and notifications keep the team in step.
Every consultation starts with context: history, allergies, vitals and structured SOAP notes.
With the patient's consent, Care Scribe transcribes the conversation, separates the speakers and drafts a clinical summary. The doctor reviews every word before signing.
Specialty tools are built in. Dentists chart findings and plan treatment on an odontogram. Eye clinics record visual acuity and refraction. Dermatology maps body sites and compares clinical photos over time. And paediatrics tracks growth charts, vaccinations and screening.
Digital prescriptions carry medication information, and every issued prescription stays on record.
The pharmacy tracks batches, expiry dates and stock levels, and dispenses first-expiry, first-out.
Lab orders move from sample collection to results and the doctor's review, without lost paperwork.
Referrals to specialists and external partners stay connected, and every one can be tracked.
Billing handles invoices, applicable GST, every payment method, receipts and balances.
At the end of the day, payments and refunds are reconciled, with GST-aware reports for clear financial oversight.
Multiple facilities run from one platform. Welbuk Cloud is available today. For on-premises, hybrid or air-gapped AI, let's talk.
For the people behind care, there are staff records, attendance, leave management and role-based access control.
After the visit, Welbuk Health keeps patients connected, with visit history, prescriptions, reports, reminders and follow-ups.
Families manage health together, with guardian links, vaccination records and growth tracking.
Looking ahead, our goal is one trusted network of clinics, specialists, pharmacies and laboratories, sharing records only with the patient's permission.
Welbuk. Technology supports the work. People deliver the care. Building a connected healthcare ecosystem.
